Feeding

Chickens need balanced poultry feed, grains, clean water, and calcium for strong eggshells. Good feeding helps hens lay better eggs.

Housing

Keep chickens in a clean, dry, and safe coop. The house should protect them from rain, cold, strong sun, and predators.

Health Care

Regular vaccination, clean bedding, fresh water, and disease control are important. Sick chickens should be separated quickly.

Benefits

Chickens provide eggs, meat, manure, and quick income. They are easy to keep and good for small farms with limited space.
