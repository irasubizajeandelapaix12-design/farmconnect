Feeding

Goats need fresh grass, clean water, leaves, hay, and mineral salts. Good feeding helps the goat grow faster and produce better milk.

Housing

Keep the goat in a clean, dry, and well-ventilated shelter. The house should protect it from rain, cold weather, and strong sun.

Health Care

Regular deworming, vaccination, and checking for wounds or sickness are important. A healthy goat is active, eats well, and has bright eyes.

Benefits

Goats are useful for milk, meat, manure, and breeding. They are also easier to keep than larger animals because they need less space.
