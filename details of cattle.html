Feeding

Cattle need fresh grass, hay, clean water, minerals, and balanced feed. Good feeding improves milk production, body weight, and general health.

Housing

Keep cattle in a clean, dry, and spacious shelter. The house should have good drainage, fresh air, and protection from heavy rain and strong sun.

Health Care

Regular vaccination, deworming, hoof care, and checking for ticks are important. Sick cattle should be separated and treated quickly.

Benefits

Cattle provide milk, meat, manure, and income for farmers. Dairy cattle can give daily milk and help a farm grow steadily.
