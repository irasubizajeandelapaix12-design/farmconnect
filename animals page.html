Animal Care Tips

Nutrition

Provide balanced feed with proteins, vitamins, and minerals. Consult vets for specific diets.

Health Management

Regular vaccinations, deworming, and health checks prevent diseases. Isolate sick animals.

Housing

Clean, ventilated shelters protect from weather and predators. Space requirements vary by species.

Breeding

Select healthy stock for breeding. Monitor gestation and provide care during birthing.
