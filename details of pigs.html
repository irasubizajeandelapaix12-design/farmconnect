Feeding

Pigs need balanced feed, grains, vegetables, clean water, and enough protein. Good feeding helps them grow fast and stay strong.

Housing

Keep pigs in a clean, dry, and strong pen with good drainage. The shelter should have enough space and protection from rain and strong sun.

Health Care

Regular deworming, clean water, clean bedding, and disease checks are important. Sick pigs should be separated and treated quickly.

Benefits

Pigs grow quickly and can bring good income for farmers. They are useful for meat production and can help expand a small farm business.
